# 22. Additional Gamification Features

These are candidates, not mandatory scope.

## Milestones

Examples:

-   First 1,000 citizens;
-   First profitable factory;
-   First railway;
-   First university;
-   First self-sufficient city;
-   First inter-city trade route;
-   First metro;
-   Zero landfill milestone.

Milestones can award Development Credits or cosmetic achievements.

## Challenges

Optional objectives can create short-term goals:

-   house 10,000 people without severe congestion;
-   achieve food self-sufficiency;
-   build a city with 80% public transport usage;
-   survive a drought;
-   connect three cities.

## Scenarios

Preconfigured worlds with constraints:

-   limited water;
-   abundant resources;
-   isolated island;
-   harsh climate;
-   rapid population growth.

## Achievements

Achievements should record interesting outcomes rather than grind.

## Statistics

Track historical records:

-   largest city;
-   highest production;
-   longest rail line;
-   most profitable business;
-   highest population growth;
-   largest disaster;
-   longest continuous simulation.

## Historical timeline

Generate a timeline of important world events.

## City identity

Cities can organically acquire traits based on their development:

-   industrial city;
-   university city;
-   farming centre;
-   logistics hub;
-   tourist destination.

These should be derived descriptors rather than fixed classes.

## News feed

A simulation news feed can turn events into concise stories:

``` text
YEAR 18 / DAY 42

Industrial output reaches a new record in Northport.
A new rail connection has reduced freight travel times by 23%.
Water demand is approaching treatment capacity.
```

## Leaderboards

For a single-player game, local historical records are preferable
initially.

Online leaderboards are optional and much later.
