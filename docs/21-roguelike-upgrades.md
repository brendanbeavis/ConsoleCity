# 21. Roguelike Upgrades and Perks

This is the initial proposed catalogue. Exact numerical values are
placeholders for balancing.

## Infrastructure

### Efficient Grid

Reduce electricity transmission losses.

### Modular Utilities

Utility upgrades are cheaper but have slightly lower maximum capacity.

### Resilient Infrastructure

Infrastructure failures are less severe and repair faster.

### Distributed Power

Small-scale generation becomes more effective.

### Water Wise

Reduce water consumption across the civilisation.

### Circular Waste

Increase recycling and reduce landfill demand.

## Transport

### Logistics Culture

Increase freight throughput.

### Transit First

Public transport attracts more users and has improved operating
efficiency.

### Road Engineering

Road construction and upgrades are cheaper.

### Emergency Priority

Emergency vehicles receive stronger route priority.

### Walkable City

Pedestrian accessibility has a larger positive effect on local
desirability.

### Freight Corridors

Designated freight routes gain capacity.

## Housing and urban development

### Modular Housing

Residential construction is faster.

### Compact City

High-density buildings require less land.

### Green Urbanism

Parks produce stronger local desirability.

### Master Planning

Infrastructure planning costs less when serving planned districts.

## Economy

### Entrepreneurial Culture

Businesses establish slightly more readily.

### Industrial Efficiency

Factories use fewer inputs.

### Local Supply Chains

Nearby suppliers receive a logistics bonus.

### Retail Networks

Commercial inventory turns over more efficiently.

### Financial Reserves

Businesses are less likely to close during short downturns.

## Agriculture

### Agricultural Science

Farm productivity increases.

### Irrigation Systems

Farms use water more efficiently.

### Mechanised Farming

Farm labour requirements decrease while equipment costs rise.

### Local Food

Local food supply chains reduce food logistics costs.

## People

### Skilled Workforce

Education produces skills faster.

### Public Health

Healthcare has stronger preventative effects.

### Education Culture

Children gain education benefits more consistently.

### Civic Pride

Population satisfaction is more resilient to temporary disruption.

## Technology

### Research Grants

Research generation increases.

### Open Standards

Technology unlock prerequisites are slightly more flexible.

### Applied Research

Technology provides stronger practical effects but costs more research.

## Events / resilience

### Disaster Planning

Natural disaster consequences are reduced.

### Emergency Stockpiles

Critical shortages take longer to develop.

### Rapid Response

Emergency services respond faster.

### Recovery Corps

Post-event reconstruction is faster.

## Specialisation perks

These are intentionally trade-off based.

### Industrial Giant

Industrial output increases, but pollution and freight demand also
increase.

### Transit Metropolis

Public transport is highly efficient, but road construction is more
expensive.

### Agricultural Heartland

Farm productivity increases, but urban land conversion becomes more
expensive.

### Knowledge Economy

Office/research output increases, but skilled-worker housing demand
rises.

### Green Civilization

Environmental quality improves faster, but some heavy industries have
higher operating costs.

## Rare perks

### Autonomous Systems

Late-game buildings can operate with fewer workers.

### National Grid

Connected power networks gain major reliability benefits.

### Universal Transit

Public transport can connect distant settlements with reduced operating
cost.

### Megacity Planning

Very high-density development becomes available.

Rare perks should be uncommon and should materially alter a run's
strategic direction.
