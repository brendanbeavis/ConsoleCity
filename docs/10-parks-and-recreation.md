# 10. Parks and Recreation

## Purpose

Parks and recreational facilities improve quality of life and create
destinations for agents.

## Basic parks

-   small park;
-   neighbourhood park;
-   regional park.

Effects:

-   recreation access;
-   land desirability;
-   local satisfaction;
-   pedestrian activity.

## Playgrounds

Primarily serve children and families.

## Sports

Facilities:

-   sports field;
-   indoor sports centre;
-   swimming pool;
-   stadium.

Effects:

-   recreation;
-   employment;
-   events;
-   local commerce.

## Major attractions

Potential later facilities:

-   aquarium;
-   zoo;
-   museum;
-   racetrack;
-   fairground.

These can attract visitors from outside the local district or city.

## Park usage

Usage depends on:

-   population nearby;
-   accessibility;
-   weather;
-   facility quality;
-   opening state;
-   preferences.

## Economic effects

Attractions can produce:

-   visitor spending;
-   transport demand;
-   hospitality demand;
-   employment.

## Planning interaction

Parks compete for land but can increase surrounding desirability.

This creates a useful planning trade-off rather than making parks
universally beneficial.
