# 15. Technology and Research

## Purpose

Technology provides long-term progression and unlocks new systems.

## Research generation

Research can come from:

-   educated population;
-   universities;
-   research organisations;
-   advanced industries;
-   player investment;
-   milestones.

## Technology tree categories

### Foundations

-   basic construction;
-   basic utilities;
-   road engineering;
-   sanitation.

### Industry

-   mechanisation;
-   advanced manufacturing;
-   automation;
-   robotics.

### Transport

-   improved roads;
-   rail;
-   electrified rail;
-   metro;
-   high-speed transport.

### Energy

-   improved generation;
-   grid optimisation;
-   renewable energy;
-   storage;
-   advanced generation.

### Agriculture

-   irrigation;
-   mechanised farming;
-   greenhouses;
-   precision agriculture.

### Computing and communications

-   telecommunications;
-   digital services;
-   advanced networks;
-   automation.

### Urban development

-   high-density construction;
-   advanced planning;
-   smart infrastructure.

## Unlock model

A technology can require:

-   research points;
-   prerequisite technologies;
-   milestone;
-   city/region scale;
-   resources.

## Technology effects

Unlocks can add:

-   new buildings;
-   new transport modes;
-   improved efficiency;
-   new production recipes;
-   improved agent behaviour;
-   new infrastructure;
-   new perks.

## Research should be meaningful

Technology should change the simulation rather than only increasing a
number.

Example:

``` text
Grid Optimisation
→ lower transmission loss
→ more effective electricity capacity
→ lower operating cost
```
