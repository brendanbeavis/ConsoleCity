# 12. Terrain and Natural Resources

## Terrain

Initial terrain types:

-   plains;
-   forest;
-   hills;
-   mountains;
-   desert;
-   coast;
-   river;
-   lake;
-   ocean.

Terrain influences development suitability.

## Natural resources

Potential resources:

-   water;
-   fertile soil;
-   timber;
-   stone;
-   sand;
-   coal;
-   iron;
-   other minerals.

## Resource deposits

A deposit has:

-   type;
-   quantity;
-   quality;
-   extraction rate;
-   location.

## Extraction

Resource industries consume:

-   land;
-   labour;
-   equipment;
-   power;
-   transport.

They produce:

-   raw materials;
-   environmental impact;
-   freight demand.

## Geography effects

Geography influences:

-   road/rail construction cost;
-   flood risk;
-   development suitability;
-   farming productivity;
-   resource availability;
-   city growth.

## Environmental modelling

Keep environmental simulation simple initially.

Track:

-   pollution;
-   water quality;
-   green space;
-   land degradation.

These values can feed back into health, desirability and productivity.
