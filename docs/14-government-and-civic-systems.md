# 14. Government and Civic Systems

## Role

Government provides public services, infrastructure, regulation and
long-term investment.

The initial player acts as the overarching civilization steward.
Detailed political simulation is optional and should not be required for
the core game.

## Government functions

-   collect revenue;
-   fund services;
-   construct public infrastructure;
-   maintain infrastructure;
-   establish zoning/planning rules;
-   respond to disasters;
-   approve major projects.

## Budget

Public budget categories:

-   infrastructure;
-   emergency services;
-   health;
-   education;
-   parks;
-   administration;
-   technology/research.

## Revenue

Potential sources:

-   property tax;
-   income tax;
-   business tax;
-   sales/consumption tax;
-   resource royalties;
-   transport fees.

Keep taxation abstract initially.

## Policies

Potential player policies:

-   development incentives;
-   industrial incentives;
-   transit funding;
-   education investment;
-   utility subsidies;
-   park funding;
-   environmental restrictions.

Policies should modify simulation parameters rather than become a large
political management subsystem.

## Civic satisfaction

A city-level civic score can be derived from:

-   safety;
-   service access;
-   employment;
-   housing affordability;
-   transport;
-   environment;
-   recreation.

The score should be explanatory, not merely a single opaque number.
