# 8. Services

## Service model

A service facility provides capacity to a population or area.

Services should have:

-   service radius/accessibility;
-   capacity;
-   staffing;
-   operating cost;
-   utility demand;
-   response/processing time;
-   quality;
-   coverage.

## Emergency services

### Police

Functions:

-   crime prevention;
-   emergency response;
-   public safety.

Inputs:

-   staff;
-   vehicles;
-   stations;
-   roads;
-   funding.

Outputs:

-   safety coverage;
-   incidents resolved.

### Fire

Functions:

-   fire response;
-   rescue;
-   disaster response.

Fire response is sensitive to road congestion and station coverage.

### Ambulance / hospital

Hospitals provide treatment capacity.

Ambulances provide transport from incidents to hospitals.

## Education

Initial levels:

-   primary school;
-   secondary school;
-   university/tertiary.

Education affects future:

-   skills;
-   employment;
-   productivity;
-   income.

## Civic services

Potential services:

-   city hall;
-   administration;
-   courts;
-   libraries;
-   community centres.

## Healthcare

Potential facilities:

-   GP/clinic;
-   hospital;
-   specialist hospital.

## Service quality

Quality can depend on:

``` text
quality =
  funding
  + staffing
  + building condition
  + technology
  + accessibility
  - overload
```

## Service interactions

Examples:

-   population growth → school demand;
-   traffic → emergency response time;
-   crime → police workload;
-   industrial pollution → health demand;
-   park availability → recreation/satisfaction.
